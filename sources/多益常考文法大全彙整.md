## 1. 多益十二大時態 (The 12 Tenses)

### 十二時態矩陣總覽表

| 時態 (Tenses) | 簡單式 (Simple) | 進行式 (Progressive) | 完成式 (Perfect) | 完成進行式 (Perfect Progressive) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **現在 (Present)** | S + V / V-es<br>*(I eat)* | S + am/is/are + V-ing<br>*(I am eating)* | S + have/has + V-p.p.<br>*(I have eaten)* | S + have/has + been + V-ing<br>*(I have been eating)* |
| **過去 (Past)** | S + V-ed<br>*(I ate)* | S + was/were + V-ing<br>*(I was eating)* | S + had + V-p.p.<br>*(I had eaten)* | S + had + been + V-ing<br>*(I had been eating)* |
| **未來 (Future)** | S + will + V<br>*(I will eat)* | S + will be + V-ing<br>*(I will be eating)* | S + will have + V-p.p.<br>*(I will have eaten)* | S + will have been + V-ing<br>*(I will have been eating)* |

---

### 各時態詳細用法與考點解析

#### 一、 過去時態系列 (Past Tenses)
1. **過去簡單式 (S + V-ed)**
   * **用法**：表達某一活動或狀態始於過去某一特定時間，並在過去結束（通常配有過去時間副詞）。
   * **從屬子句時態一致**：主要動詞為過去式時，從屬子句通常也用過去式或過去完成式。
   * **例句**：
     * *He often brought his lunch to work.*
     * *Tina had hamburgers for lunch every day last week.*
     * *I thought she was a student.*
2. **過去進行式 (S + was/were + V-ing)**
   * **用法**：表示在過去某個特定時間點，某動作正在進行中。
   * **與過去簡單式連用**：描述「當一個短動作發生時，另一個長動作正在進行」。長動作用進行式，短動作用簡單式。若兩動作時間長短相當且同時進行，則皆用進行式或簡單式。
   * **例句**：
     * *While I was studying in one room, my roommate was having a party in the other.*
     * *When I got to his home on Sunday, he was making tea.*
3. **過去完成式 (S + had + V-p.p.)**
   * **用法**：表過去發生的兩個事件中，「先發生」的事件用過去完成式，「後發生」的用過去簡單式。若句中有 `before` 或 `after` 清晰標示前後，有時可用簡單過去式替代。
   * **常見副詞**：`ever`, `never`, `still`, `just`, `already`, `yet`。
   * **例句**：
     * *Sam had already left by the time Ann got there.*
     * *The professor had already given a test when I got to class.*
4. **過去完成進行式 (S + had been + V-ing)**
   * **用法**：在過去某事件發生之前，更早的另一動作已持續進行了一段時間且仍在持續；或用來表示過去事件的因果關係。
   * **例句**：
     * *Eric finally came at 18:00. I had been waiting for him since 16:30.*
     * *Amy gained weight because she had been overeating.*

#### 二、 現在時態系列 (Present Tenses)
1. **現在簡單式 (S + V/V-es)**
   * **用法**：陳述客觀事實、真理、習慣性活動或目前狀況。
   * **例句**：
     * *Water consists of hydrogen and oxygen.*
     * *I drink coffee every morning.*
2. **現在進行式 (S + am/is/are + V-ing)**
   * **用法**：
     1. 說話當下正在進行的動作，或近期的暫時性活動。
     2. 代替未來式（常用於來去動詞如 `come`, `arrive`, `go`, `leave`, `quit`）。
     3. 搭配 `always`, `constantly` 表示經常發生且令人不快的習慣。
   * **例句**：
     * *I'm quitting my job next Friday.*
     * *He is being foolish.* (表示暫時性的行為，非永久性格)
3. **現在完成式 (S + have/has + V-p.p.)**
   * **用法**：表示發生於過去但確切時間不詳的經驗、重複發生的活動，或搭配 `for`/`since` 表從過去持續至今的狀態。
   * **例句**：
     * *I have already seen that movie.*
     * *We have had four tests so far this year.*
     * *I have known him for many years.*
4. **現在完成進行式 (S + have/has been + V-ing)**
   * **用法**：強調從過去持續至今且「仍在進行」的活動，常與 `for`, `since`, `all day`, `all week` 連用。
   * **例句**：
     * *I have been thinking about changing my major.*
     * *For the last two months, I have been working as a waitress.*

#### 三、 未來時態系列 (Future Tenses)
1. **未來簡單式 (S + will + V / S + be going to + V)**
   * **will vs. be going to 比較**：
     * `be going to + V`：先前已決定並規劃好的打算。
     * `will + V`：當下決定、未定時間的意願或純粹客觀預測。
   * **例句**：
     * *I am going to paint my bedroom tomorrow.* (已規劃)
     * *According to the weather report, it will be cloudy tomorrow.* (預測)
2. **未來進行式 (S + will be + V-ing)**
   * **用法**：表未來某一特定時間點正在進行的活動，或按慣例/安排會發生的事。
   * **副詞子句時態**：在時間或條件連接詞（`when`, `after`, `before`, `if`, `as soon as`, `until`）引導的子句中，用**現在簡單式**代替未來式。
   * **例句**：
     * *I will be studying when you come.*
     * *When Bill gets up tomorrow morning, the sun will be shining.*
3. **未來完成式 (S + will have + V-p.p.)**
   * **用法**：表某一活動將在未來某特定時間或事件發生「之前」已經完成。
   * **例句**：
     * *By the time I see you in July, I will have graduated.*
     * *I will have lived here for ten years by the end of next month.*
4. **未來完成進行式 (S + will have been + V-ing)**
   * **用法**：強調某一活動在未來某時間點前，已經「持續進行」了多久。
   * **例句**：
     * *When Prof. Jones retires next month, he will have been teaching for 45 years.*
     * *Nancy will have been waiting for an hour by the time we get there.*

## 2. 持續性與非持續性動詞 (Dynamic vs. Stative Verbs)

### 動詞屬性與完成式考點
* **持續性動詞 (Dynamic/Continuous Verbs)**：動作可長時間延續。
  * *常見動詞*：`learn`, `work`, `stand`, `know`, `wait`, `read`, `sleep`, `live`, `stay` 等。
* **非持續性/瞬間動詞 (Non-Continuous/Stative/Momentary Verbs)**：動作在瞬間完成，無法持續。
  * *常見動詞*：`allow`, `open`, `kill`, `return`, `leave`, `die`, `begin`, `finish`, `come`, `go`, `buy`, `graduate`, `catch` 等。

### 多益常見錯點對照

| 錯誤用法 (Incorrect) | 正確用法 (Correct) | 觀念說明 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ❌ She has died for three years. | ⭕ She has **been dead** for three years.<br>⭕ She died three years ago. | `die` 為瞬間動作，不能配合 `for + 時間` 表示持續狀態；應改用狀態形容詞 `dead`。 |
| ❌ How long have you bought the car? | ⭕ How long have you **had** the car? | `buy` 為瞬間動作，表達持有該車多久應用 `have`。 |
| ❌ He has caught a cold for a week. | ⭕ He has **had** a cold for a week. | `catch a cold` 是染上的瞬間，持續感冒狀態應用 `have a cold`。 |

## 3. 被動語態 (Passive Voice)

### 基本結構與九大時態被動式對照表

基本結構：**主詞 + be 動詞 + 過去分詞 (V-p.p.) (+ by + 動作執行者)**

| 時態 | 主動語態 (Active) | 被動語態 (Passive) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **現在簡單式** | She buys the apples. | The apples **are bought** by her. |
| **過去簡單式** | She bought the apples. | The apples **were bought** by her. |
| **未來簡單式** | She will buy the apples. | The apples **will be bought** by her. |
| **現在完成式** | She has bought the apples. | The apples **have been bought** by her. |
| **過去完成式** | She had bought the apples. | The apples **had been bought** by her. |
| **未來完成式** | She will have bought the apples. | The apples **will have been bought** by her. |
| **現在進行式** | She is buying the apples. | The apples **are being bought** by her. |
| **過去進行式** | She was buying the apples. | The apples **were being bought** by her. |
| **含助動詞** | She would buy the apples. | The apples **would be bought** by her. |

### 主動改被動三大原則
1. 將主動句的**受詞**改為被動句的**主詞**。
2. 將主要動詞改為 **「be 動詞 + V-p.p.」**，be 動詞的單複數與時態須隨新主詞調整。
3. 將原主詞置於介系詞 `by` 之後，若動作執行者不重要或不明確時可省略。

### 特殊被動用法與注意事項
1. **虛主詞 It 的被動結構**：某些表示觀點、信念的動詞常搭配虛主詞：
   * *It is believed that she is ill.*
   * *It is reported / said / expected that...*
2. **連綴動詞 + V-p.p. (形容詞補語，屬主動狀態)**：
   * 結構看起來像被動，但其實是 $S + V + C$ 結構，V-p.p. 已轉化為形容詞用以補充說明主詞狀態。
   * *Jennifer is married.* (Jennifer 結婚了——形容詞補語)
   * *My microwave oven is broken.* (微波爐壞了——形容詞補語)
3. **慣用被動動詞片語**：
   * *Business was slack and many employees **were let go**.* (被解僱)

## 4. Should 省略句型 (Subjunctive Mood)

在表示「主張、要求、建議、命令、決定、重要性」等意志概念的句型中，`that` 子句內的動詞須用**原形動詞 (VR)**，其原因為助動詞 `should` 被省略。

### 一、 常見意志動詞 (Verb + that + S + (should) + VR)
* **公式**：`S + 動詞 + that + S + (should) + 原形 V`
* **高頻動詞分類**：
  * **主張/堅持**：`insist`, `maintain`, `urge`
  * **要求**：`ask`, `demand`, `request`, `require`, `beg`
  * **建議/提議**：`suggest`, `recommend`, `propose`, `advise`, `move`
  * **命令/決定/指示**：`order`, `command`, `decide`, `direct`, `instruct`, `specify`, `provide` (規定)
* **名詞衍生形同理**：`suggestion`, `proposal`, `demand`, `request`, `requirement`, `insistence`, `order`, `recommendation` 等名詞後面的 `that` 同位語子句亦適用此法則。

### 二、 常見意志形容詞 (It is + Adj + that + S + (should) + VR)
* **公式**：`It is + 形容詞 + that + S + (should) + 原形 V`
* **高頻形容詞**：`important`, `necessary`, `essential`, `vital`, `crucial`, `imperative` (必要的/緊急的), `urgent`, `desirable`, `advisable` (明智的), `compulsory` (強制性的), `appropriate`。

### 三、 被動語態注意事項
若 `that` 子句內為被動語態，應保留 **`be + V-p.p.`**，其中 `be` 絕對不可省略或改為 `is/are/was`！

### 實戰句型範例
1. *The manager insisted that the impractical project **(should) be cancelled**.* (經理堅持要取消該計畫——被動語態)
2. *It was urgent that the patient **(should) be taken** to the hospital immediately.*
3. *It is recommended that the project **not be started** until all preparations are made.* (否定句將 `not` 放於 `be` 前)
4. *It is my insistence that she **leave** in no time.* (第三人稱單數仍用原形 `leave`)

## 5. 關係代名詞與關係子句 (Relative Pronouns & Clauses)

關係代名詞兼具「代名詞」與「連接詞」雙重功能，用來引導形容詞子句修飾先行詞。

### 關係代名詞人稱與格位總表

| 先行詞類別 | 主格 (Subject) | 受格 (Object) | 所有格 (Possessive) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **人 (People)** | who, that | whom, that, who | whose |
| **事物 / 動物 (Things/Animals)** | which, that | which, that | whose, of which |
| **人 + 事物 / 特殊限定** | that | that | — |

*註：`that` 前面**絕對不能**有逗號 `,` 或介系詞！*

---

### 限定性與非限定性關係子句

| 特性比較 | 限定性子句 (Restrictive) | 非限定性子句 (Non-Restrictive) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **標點符號** | 無逗號 `,` | 主句與關頭之間有逗號 `,` |
| **主要功能** | 限定先行詞範圍，屬於**必要資訊**；刪除後句意不完整或改變。 | 補充說明已知獨一無二的人事物；刪除後主句意思依然完整。 |
| **that 的使用** | 可使用 `that` | **嚴禁使用 `that`** |
| **關代能否省略** | 受格關代可省略 | **關代絕對不能省略** |

#### 經典對照範例：
1. **限定用法 (有多個選擇)**：
   * *Mr. Wang has a son **who lives in Taichung**.*
     * (王先生有不只一個兒子，這句限定是「住在台中的那一個兒子」。)
   * *Brian has a cat **which can sleep all night**.* (布萊恩有多隻貓)
2. **非限定用法 (同位語概念，補充說明)**：
   * *Mr. Wang has a son**, who lives in Taichung**.*
     * (王先生只有一個兒子，補充說明他住台中。)
   * *Teresa**, who is good at English,** is thinking about starting a cram school.*
   * *The article**, which appeared today in Taipei Times,** is very interesting.*

## 6. 假設語氣全解析 (Conditionals & Subjunctive Mood)

### 一、 基本四大條件句 (Standard Conditionals)

| 類別 | If 子句時態 | 主要子句時態 | 語意與時機 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Zero Conditional**<br>(習慣/真理) | 現在簡單式 | 現在簡單式 | 表事實、科學真理或普遍習慣。<br>例：*If iron gets wet, it rusts.* |
| **First Conditional**<br>(現在/未來可能) | 現在簡單式 | will / can / may / might + VR | 未來有可能實現的情況。<br>例：*If you don't hurry, you might miss the flight.* |
| **Second Conditional**<br>(與現在事實相反) | 過去簡單式<br>(be 動詞一律用 `were`) | would / could / might + VR | 與現在事實相反或極不可能。<br>例：*If I were you, I wouldn't do that.* |
| **Third Conditional**<br>(與過去事實相反) | 過去完成式<br>(had + V-p.p.) | would / could / might + have + V-p.p. | 對過去已發生事實的假設與後悔。<br>例：*If I had studied hard, I would have passed.* |

---

### 二、 混合假設語氣 (Mixed Conditionals)

當條件子句與主要子句發生的時間軸不一致時，使用混合假設語氣：

1. **If + 談論過去，與現在相關**：
   * 結構：`If + S + had + V-p.p., S + would/could/might + VR` (主要子句常見 `now`, `today`)
   * 例：*If my uncle had worked hard, he would be a rich man now.*
2. **If + 談論過去，與未來相關**：
   * 結構：`If + S + had + V-p.p., S + would be + V-ing / would + VR`
   * 例：*If she had signed up last week, she would be joining us tomorrow.*
3. **If + 談論現在，與過去相關**：
   * 結構：`If + S + were/過去式, S + would/could + have + V-p.p.`
   * 例：*If I were rich, I would have bought that Ferrari we saw yesterday.*
4. **If + 談論現在，與未來相關**：
   * 結構：`If + S + were/過去式, S + would + VR`
   * 例：*If Sam weren't nice, he wouldn't be tutoring you tonight.*
5. **If + 談論未來，與過去相關**：
   * 結構：`If + S + were V-ing, S + would have + V-p.p.`
   * 例：*If I weren't going on a business trip next week, I would have accepted the new assignment.*
6. **If + 談論未來，與現在相關**：
   * 結構：`If + S + were V-ing, S + would + VR`
   * 例：*If I were going to that concert tonight, I would be very excited.*

---

### 三、 特殊假設句型與倒裝

1. **若非 / 要不是 (If it were not for...)**
   * **與現在事實相反**：`If it were not for + N, S + would + VR`
     * 等於：`Were it not for + N` / `But for + N` / `Without + N`
     * 例：*If it were not for oxygen, no man could live.*
   * **與過去事實相反**：`If it had not been for + N, S + would have + V-p.p.`
     * 等於：`Had it not been for + N` / `But for + N` / `Without + N`
     * 例：*But for your advice, I should have failed.*
2. **對未來極不可能發生之推測 (Were to)**
   * 結構：`If + S + were to + VR, S + would/could/might + VR`
   * 用於表示極不可能或災難性的情況。
   * 例：*If the sun were to rise in the west, I would go out with you.*
3. **萬一 / 碰巧 (Should / Happen to)**
   * 結構：`If + S + should / happen to + VR, S + will/would + VR`
   * 例：*If there should be strikes, the company will lay off workers.*

#### 四、 省略 If 的倒裝句型 (Inversion)
將 `If` 省略後，助動詞或 `Were` / `Had` / `Should` 須移至主詞前面：
* **與現在相反 (Were)**：`Were I young, I would learn to ski.` (← If I were young...)
* **萬一 (Should)**：`Should it rain, they would not come.` (← If it should rain...)
* **與過去相反 (Had)**：`Had I been there, I would have helped you.` (← If I had been there...)

---

### 五、 Wish 的假設語氣用法

`wish` 句型用以表達「與事實相反且無法實現的願望」，`that` 子句的時態不受 `wish` 本身時態影響：

* **與現在事實相反**：`S + wish + (that) + S + 過去式動詞 (be動詞一律用 were)`
  * 例：*I wish I were a bird.* / *I wish you would stop complaining.*
* **與過去事實相反**：`S + wish + (that) + S + had + V-p.p.`
  * 例：*I wish I had passed the test.* (但當時沒通過)
  * 例：*I was born in 2025, but I wish I'd grown up in the 1980s.*
* **與未來事實相反/難以實現**：`S + wish + (that) + S + would/could + VR`

## 7. 動名詞與不定詞 (Gerunds & Infinitives)

### 一、 to 為介係詞（後接 V-ing 或 N）的關鍵片語表

多益極常考 `to` 作為介係詞的結構，後面不可接原形動詞，必須接 **V-ing** 或 **名詞**：

| 片語 | 中文釋義 | 片語 | 中文釋義 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **look forward to V-ing** | 期待 | **be/get used to V-ing** | 習慣於 |
| **be committed to V-ing** | 致力於/承諾 | **be accustomed to V-ing** | 習慣於 |
| **object to V-ing** | 反對 | **be opposed to V-ing** | 反對 |
| **contribute to V-ing** | 貢獻/導致 | **lead to V-ing** | 導致 |
| **with a view to V-ing** | 為了... | **when it comes to V-ing** | 一談到...時 |
| **pay attention to V-ing** | 注意 | **admit / confess to V-ing** | 承認 |
| **be adapted to V-ing** | 適合於 | **adjust to V-ing** | 調整以適應 |
| **the key to V-ing** | ...的關鍵 | **in addition to V-ing** | 除...之外 |
| **give rise to V-ing** | 引起 | **with regard / reference to V-ing**| 關於 |

*其餘接 V-ing 的常見片語*：
* `cannot help + V-ing` (= `cannot help but + VR`) 忍不住...
* `There is no use + V-ing` (= `It is of no use to + VR`) 做...是無用的
* `have trouble / difficulty + (in) V-ing` 做...有困難

### 二、 後面只接 V-ing 的動詞 (含速記口訣)

`avoid`, `admit`, `appreciate`, `consider`, `delay`, `deny`, `enjoy`, `finish`, `mind`, `postpone`, `practice`, `quit`, `recommend`, `resist`, `risk`, `suggest`, `include`, `anticipate`, `discuss`.

> **速記口訣**：
> * 堅持練習必完成，避免延期勿介意 (`keep`, `practice`, `complete`, `avoid`, `postpone`, `mind`)
> * 想像包含著預期，討論提議來考慮 (`imagine`, `include`, `anticipate`, `discuss`, `suggest`, `consider`)
> * 承認厭惡不否定，禁止辭去做保證 (`admit`, `resent`, `deny`, `forbid`, `quit`, `ensure`)
> * 放棄享受免後悔，結束冒險真感謝 (`give up`, `enjoy`, `regret`, `finish`, `risk`, `appreciate`)

### 三、 後面只接 To-V 的動詞 (含速記口訣)

`agree`, `afford`, `decide`, `expect`, `hope`, `intend`, `manage`, `offer`, `plan`, `promise`, `refuse`, `seem`, `want`, `would like`, `fail`, `prepare`, `tend`.

> **速記口訣**：
> 想要要求你的同意，將你決定的計劃告訴我；希望你有需要時可以邀請我，不要故意忘了我。

### 四、 兩者皆可接，但語意不同

* **To-V** 代表「未來的目標 / 尚未做的事」。
* **V-ing** 代表「已做過的事 / 持續的動作或狀態」。

| 動詞 | + To-V (未做/未來目標) | + V-ing (已做/持續狀態) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **remember** | 記得去做某事 (*Please remember to write.*) | 記得曾做過某事 (*I remember writing.*) |
| **forget** | 忘記要做某事 (*I forgot to call.*) | 忘記曾做過某事 (*I forgot calling.*) |
| **regret** | 遺憾地通知/去做 (*Regret to tell you...*) | 後悔做了某事 (*Regret telling you...*) |
| **stop** | 停下手中事去**做另一件事** (*Stop to smoke*) | **停止**正在做的事 (*Stop smoking*) |
| **try** | 盡力、努力嘗試去達成 (*Try to pass*) | 嘗試某種方法試試看 (*Try changing batteries*) |
| **continue** | 中斷後繼續 (*Continued to play after lunch*) | 無間斷一直持續 (*Continued playing for 3 hours*) |

#### Need 的主被動用法比較：
* **事物 + need + V-ing** (表被動意義) = **事物 + need + to be + V-p.p.**
  * *My car needs **washing**.* = *My car needs **to be washed**.*
  * *The garden needs **weeding**.* = *The garden needs **to be weeded**.*

### 五、 Used to 核心三兄弟辨析

| 結構 | 詞性與解析 | 中文意思 | 例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **S + used to + VR** | `used` 為助動詞/過去動詞，後接原形 V | 過去經常...（現已不復存在） | *He **used to** smoke.*<br>否定：*He didn't use to smoke.* |
| **物 + be used to + VR** | `used` 為 `use` 的被動語態，`to` 為不定詞 | 某物被用來做... | *The tool **is used to** fix bikes.* |
| **人 + be/get used to + V-ing/N** | `to` 為介係詞，後接名詞或動名詞 | 習慣於... / 逐漸習慣... | *He has **gotten used to** the noise.* |

## 8. 分詞與分詞構句 (Participles & Participial Constructions)

### 一、 主動分詞 (V-ing) 與被動分詞 (V-p.p.) 的三大差異

1. **進行 vs. 完成**：
   * *falling leaves* (正在落下的葉子) vs. *fallen leaves* (地上的落葉)
   * *boiling water* (滾騰中的水) vs. *boiled water* (煮開過的水)
2. **特性 vs. 心情 (情緒動詞)**：
   * *The movie was touching.* (電影令人感動——特性 V-ing)
   * *I was disappointed.* (我感到失望——心情 V-p.p.)
3. **主動 vs. 被動**：
   * *a letter written by the principal* (被校長寫的信——被動 V-p.p.)
   * *the girl talking with Mr. Wang* (正與王先生說話的女孩——主動 V-ing)

---

### 二、 分詞構句簡化 5 步驟

當兩個子句的主詞相同時，可將次要子句簡化為分詞構句：
1. 確認兩子句的主詞一致。
2. 去掉連接詞 (如 `when`, `because`, `after`)。
3. 去掉次要子句的主詞。
4. 將動詞改成分詞：**主動改 V-ing**，**被動改 V-p.p.** (原 `being + V-p.p.` 的 `being` 通常省略)。
5. 若為否定句，將 `not` 或 `never` 放於分詞最前面。

#### 形容詞子句簡化為分詞片語（減縮子句）：
* 原句：*The panel **which is composed of** five scholars is commissioned...*
* 簡化：1) 去掉關代 `which` → 2) `is` 改 `being` → 3) 省略 `being`
* 結果：*The panel **composed of** five scholars is commissioned...*

---

### 三、 情境與考題實戰分析

#### 情境範例分析（Ian 的故事）：
* *Waking to the buzz of the alarm clock, Ian cursed...* (Ian 自己醒來 → 主動 **V-ing**)
* *Thinking of his unfinished project, he jumped...* (Ian 想到... → 主動 **V-ing**)
* *Stepping into the bathroom, he tripped...* (踏進浴室 → 主動 **V-ing**)
* *Helped by his mother, Ian got up...* (被母親扶起 → 被動 **V-p.p.**)

#### 多益考題實戰演練：
* **例 1**：*Jane Austen wrote Pride and Prejudice when she was 21, ______ her one of the youngest writers of a literary masterpiece.*
  * (A) was made  (B) **making**  (C) made  (D) has made
  * **解析**：原句為 `which makes her...`，簡化關代後改為現在分詞 **making**，故選 (B)。
* **例 2**：*Some of the experiments ______ in the book are easy to perform.*
  * (A) describing  (B) **described**  (C) to be described  (D) to describe
  * **解析**：原句為 `experiments which are described...`，被動結構簡化後保留過去分詞 **described**，故選 (B)。
